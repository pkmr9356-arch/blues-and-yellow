## Goal
Transform the Blues&Yellow homepage hero into an unmistakably premium, editorial "sentence-as-layout" composition, while strictly protecting and elevating the GSAP horizontal-scroll section.

1. **Hero Redesign**:

2. **Elevate and Protect Horizontal Scroll**:
